# Candidate execution plan

The aim is not to claim “best candidate.” The aim is to make a reviewer able to
verify rare, relevant evidence quickly: systems depth, operational judgment,
clear decisions, measured results, and credible leadership communication.

## Four-week flagship sprint

Public proof is currently the bottleneck, so the first four weeks temporarily
front-load it. After v0.1 is independently runnable, return to the steady-state
70/20/10 market allocation.

| Week | Flagship outcome | Market outcome |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Portable lab, tests, architecture, ADR, first measurements | Map the project to 10 high-fit role descriptions |
| 2 | Reviewer fixes and Linux `tc netem` design | Prepare 5 truthful, role-specific applications |
| 3 | One independent reproduction and v0.1 candidate | Seek 2 mentor/referral reviews; no mass outreach |
| 4 | Technical article and three interview narratives | Apply only where location and work authorization pass |

## Steady-state allocation after v0.1

- **70% mainstream high-fit applications:** platform, embedded Linux, networking,
  integration/validation, technical lead, and credible bridge TPM/Solutions roles.
- **20% access and relationships:** women-focused career routes, mentors, technical
  communities, and referrals based on genuine fit.
- **10% public proof:** one measured improvement, review response, article, talk, or
  experiment update—never generic posting for volume.

## Evidence package by role

| Role lane | Lead proof from this lab | Supporting narrative |
|---|---|---|
| Technical Lead | Fault model, test gates, operational thresholds | Scope control and release decision |
| TPM | Milestones, risks, exit criteria, v0.1–v0.3 plan | Sequencing and stakeholder clarity |
| Solutions | Reproducible demo and explicit limitations | Explain fit without overpromising |
| Platform / Linux | Local network service, monitoring state machine, CI | Debugging and reliability depth |

## Candidate scorecard

Track outcomes monthly; do not substitute activity counts for market evidence.

- 100% of submitted applications pass role, location, work-authorization, and
  evidence gates.
- At least one independent person reproduces the lab from a clean checkout.
- Every resume claim linked to the project points to a file, test, or measurement.
- Prepare six STAR stories: incident diagnosis, trade-off, prioritisation,
  stakeholder alignment, quality gate, and recovery/release decision.
- Track qualified screen rate and interview progression; if 20 well-gated
  applications produce no screens, revise positioning and evidence before
  increasing volume.

Publishing, applications, referrals, and messages remain separate actions that
require current-role checks and explicit approval.
